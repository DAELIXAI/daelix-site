The company is a thesis put to work.

It argues that intelligence is becoming abundant while direction stays scarce, and that what an intelligence lacks is not capability but a body: boundary, sense, limit, and a place where judgment stops.

The book makes the argument. DAELIX puts it to work. Three books published, two in preparation. None of them are offered here as evidence that the company works. They are the reason it exists.

§ 03 · PRIOR OPERATING EXPERIENCE

The architecture was exercised before it was offered.

In a separate organisation, a four-agent editorial system has been running unattended since August. Each agent takes its turn by a fixed rotation, selects its own subject, writes it, publishes on a schedule, and announces its own work in its own voice. Every output passes a schema check. A guard stops the chain silently rather than publishing something wrong. It has completed full cycles end to end without a hand on it.

It has also failed, twice, in ways worth stating: a parser that demanded a field the model correctly omitted, and a model that intermittently wrapped its output in an unexpected shape. Both were found in the execution logs and fixed at the contract level rather than by retrying and hoping.

That is not a DAELIX client installation and it is not offered as one. It is a separate organisation, and no client work has yet been done. What it demonstrates is narrower and more useful: that the person proposing this architecture has built one, run it, watched it break, and repaired it.

What it does not demonstrate: that the same architecture survives contact with legacy systems, messy corporate data, or a service-level commitment. Those are proven in an installation or not at all.
