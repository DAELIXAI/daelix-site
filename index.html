WHO THIS IS FOR

For businesses whose operation has outgrown the way it is being run.

The unit is the workflow. The diagnosis is the whole operation. We do not begin with a request to automate a task. We begin by examining how work actually moves through your business: where information enters, where decisions are made, where people repeatedly intervene, where systems fail to speak to each other, where knowledge disappears, and where human attention is consumed by work that no longer requires it.

STUDY 003

Composite resemblance, and the gate that came out of it

Study 002 exposed a hole: a system can clear every individual rule and still produce something that, taken whole, is a copy of a thing it was never allowed to copy. The correction is a gate that forces the system to name what its own output resembles, in writing, before the output is permitted to exist, and to record the verdict either way. It is now installed and it is not optional.

STANDING TRIAL

An autonomous editorial system, running on schedule since May

In a separate organisation, a multi-agent system researches, writes, publishes and distributes on a fixed schedule with no human in the loop. It is the longest-running instance of this architecture we operate, and it is where the cell was proven before anyone was asked to buy it.

It has also failed, and the failure is the point. For eight consecutive days the system published correctly to the site and silently did not distribute. Every call returned success. The cause was a single stale identifier pointing at a channel that had been retired. It was found by reading the record back rather than trusting the response, and the pipeline now refuses to complete unless distribution is verified on read-back.
